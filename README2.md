# YouTube Search Bar

A beautiful, customizable search page for YouTube — just open it and search. No sign-ups, no tracking, no servers. Everything lives on your device.


---

## What This Is

YouTube Search Bar is a personal homepage for searching YouTube. Instead of going to youtube.com every time, you open this page, type what you're looking for, and get redirected directly to YouTube results.

But it's more than just a search box. You can make it truly yours — change colors, pick a visual style, upload your own background image, and adjust the font size. Everything you customize is saved automatically on your computer, so it looks the same every time you open it.

---

## Features

- **Search YouTube instantly** — Type and press Enter, or click the search button. That's it.
- **Three unique visual styles** — Retro (neon/arcade vibe), Minimal (clean and modern), or Hand-drawn (playful sketchbook look).
- **Light & Dark modes** — Every style has both a light and a dark version.
- **Custom accent colors** — Choose your own highlight color for buttons and interactions.
- **Smart color system** — Pick a design color and the page automatically adjusts backgrounds, text, borders, and more to match.
- **Upload your own background** — Use any image as your page background, with controls for blur and overlay darkness.
- **Adjustable font size** — Make text bigger or smaller to suit your eyes.
- **Personal greeting** — Enter your name and the page will welcome you.
- **Search history** — Your recent searches appear below the search bar. Click any past search to run it again. Delete individual items or clear everything.
- **Filter-as-you-type** — Start typing in the search box and your history instantly filters to show matching past searches.
- **Private by design** — No accounts, no data collection, no servers. Everything is stored in your browser on your own device.
- **Works anywhere** — Open the file in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge — any modern browser, on any device.

## How to Get Started

1. **Download** the `youtube_searchbar.html` file.
2. **Double-click** it to open in your web browser.
3. **Start searching** — type anything and press Enter.

That's all. No installation, no setup, no internet required (except for the actual YouTube search, of course).

## How to Use

### Searching
Type your query in the search bar and press **Enter** or click the **search icon** button. You'll be taken to YouTube with your results ready.

### Switching Styles
Click the **Retro**, **Minimal**, or **Hand-drawn** buttons at the top of the page to instantly change the look and feel.

### Toggling Light/Dark Mode
Click the **Light** or **Dark** button (top-right corner) to switch between modes.

### Opening Settings
Click the **Settings** button (top-right corner) to open the customization panel. From there you can:

- Enter your name for a personalized greeting
- Change the design color (the main color palette for the active style)
- Change the accent color (used for buttons and highlights)
- Adjust the font size
- Upload a background image
- Control background blur and overlay darkness
- Find social links and more

### Using Search History
Your past searches appear as clickable "bubbles" below the search bar. You can:

- **Click a bubble** to search that term again
- **Click the ×** on a bubble to remove it from history
- **Click "Clear All"** to delete your entire search history
- **Click "Hide"** to collapse the history section

### Changing Colors
- **Accent Color** — Controls the red highlight color used for buttons and interactions.
- **Design Color** — Controls the main text color of the active style. The page automatically generates a full matching color palette from this one color. Each visual style remembers its own design color.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this a real YouTube product?**
No. This is an independent, open-source project. It simply redirects your searches to YouTube's search results page.

**Do you collect my data?**
No. The app stores everything locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere. You can verify this yourself — the entire app is one file with no external connections.

**Do I need to install anything?**
No. Just open the HTML file in any browser.

**Does it work on mobile?**
Yes. The page is designed to work on phones, tablets, and desktops.

**Can I reset everything?**
Clearing your browser's data for this page (or using your browser's "Clear Site Data" option) will reset all settings and history.
